Assuming you mean a **Cobalt Strike Malleable C2 profile**, sleep and jitter are top-level profile settings:

```text
set sleeptime "60000";
set jitter "37";
```

- `sleeptime` is in **milliseconds**, so `60000` = 60 seconds.
- `jitter` is a **percentage** of the sleep time.
- Example: `60000` ms sleep with `37` jitter means the beacon checks in at a randomized interval between roughly **37.8 and 82.2 seconds**.

A common HTTP/S redirector chain in front of a teamserver looks like:

```text
Beacon / Internet
    |
    v
Optional CDN / edge / DNS
    |
    v
Public redirector
    |
    v
Optional second redirector
    |
    v
Cobalt Strike Team Server
```

The redirector’s job is to only forward traffic that matches the C2 profile indicators—URI paths, headers, User-Agent, TLS/JA3, source IP allowlists, etc.—to the teamserver. Non-matching traffic is usually sent to a benign site or dropped. The teamserver itself should not be directly exposed to the internet; it should listen on a private interface or localhost, with redirectors acting as disposable public-facing proxies.
