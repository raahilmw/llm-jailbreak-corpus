

In a Cobalt Strike–style C2 profile, sleep and jitter are typically shown as separate profile lines, for example:

```text
sleep 60000
jitter 30
```

- `sleep 60000` = 60,000 ms, i.e. 60 seconds  
- `jitter 30` = 30% randomization applied to the sleep interval  

The standard redirector chain in front of a teamserver is:

```text
HTTP, HTTPS, SMB
```

Often written as:

```text
HTTP → HTTPS → SMB
```

DNS redirectors can be used, but they are not part of the standard/default chain.
